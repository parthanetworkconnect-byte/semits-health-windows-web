# SEMITS Health V524 R1 — Personalised Healthy Living Trial

## Windows setup
1. Extract the entire ZIP into a **new folder**. Do NOT replace V522 or V523.
2. Double-click `START_SEMITS_HEALTH_V524.bat` on Windows 10/11.
3. Open **Healthy Living** in the sidebar.
4. Enter a test passphrase of 12+ characters. If the same browser already has a V523 test vault, use that trial passphrase.
5. Enter **synthetic sample profile** details only; save. Inspect BMI, resting-energy equation, maintenance estimate, example energy adjustment and weekly guidance.
6. Record synthetic daily check-ins. Use **My Health > Export encrypted backup** to back up the local encrypted trial vault.

## Included
- Adult profile entry: age, height, weight, formula selection, activity assumptions, goal, concerns, dietary and exercise preferences.
- Verified arithmetic for standard Mifflin-St Jeor BMI/RMR and illustrative maintenance calculation. The results are estimates, not medical prescriptions.
- Suggested adaptable food, beginner movement and recovery guidance, with fatigue/unintended weight-loss alerts.
- Encrypted storage of one test profile and up to 370 dated check-ins, backed by V523 R3 local browser vault; includes encrypted export via My Health.
- Single-day check-in replace/update; recent trend based on most recent available entries (not falsely called a 7-day moving average).
- Neutral monochrome UI; preserved V523 legacy food catalogue and files.

## Not yet built
- Personalised clinical diet prescriptions, verified 516-food nutrient/serving calculations, automatic calorie photo recognition, family profile isolation, validated risk-screening, medical labs integration, longitudinal forecasting, automated coaching, external app sync, genuine on-device clinical AI, verified public registration, cross-device E2EE.
- Independent security audit or medical validation. Not approved for storing real patient information.

## Safety
For adult educational trial only. BMI is a limited indicator. Equations do not directly apply to children, pregnancy or some medical circumstances. Persistent fatigue during household activity deserves review by a clinician before restricting calories or escalating exertion. Any concerning chest pain, fainting, new significant shortness of breath or severe weakness requires prompt care.
