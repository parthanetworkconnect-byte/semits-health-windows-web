# SEMITS Health V9.13 – Global Food Detail Expansion

## What changed
- Retained all 561 catalogue entries and existing SEMITS Health modules.
- Added representative illustrated food photography across fish, seafood, meats, legumes and grain categories. These are representative, NOT photos of the exact species or product.
- Expanded rounded indicative per-100 g nutrition entries: 56 newly populated generic samples (plus the original 27). These are **not exact food-ID-verified values**. Fat appears for samples where recorded; unknown fat stays blank.
- Expanded food-group-specific guidance for benefits, digestion, allergens, safety, farm/origin, and animal exposure. These are evidence-aware educational summaries, not individual clinical claims.
- 195-country explorer, medical reports, gym and other modules retained.

## Limitations
- The 561 entries do not yet have verified nutrient IDs, complete species-specific photos, exact cultivar sources, micronutrients, organic certificates or individualized metabolism measurements.
- Most categories display *representative* illustrations, never presented as exact species photographs.
- Avoid using this app for allergy clearances, pet feeding decisions, medication changes or diagnosing disease.

## Run
1. Back up data in your older app. 2. Close its server. 3. Extract this ZIP. 4. Double-click START_WINDOWS.bat. 5. Visit http://127.0.0.1:8773 and press Ctrl+F5.

## Authoritative sources for the next data-import phase
- USDA FoodData Central: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/
- FAO/INFOODS country food tables: https://www.fao.org/food-composition/tables-and-databases/en/
- Food Standards Australia New Zealand: https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/

Source pages are linked as data resources; they do not prove that each illustrated sample is matched to an authoritative individual food ID.
