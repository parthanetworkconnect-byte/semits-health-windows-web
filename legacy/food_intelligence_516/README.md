# SEMITS Food Intelligence V519 — Integrated Offline Application

Open `SEMITS_Food_Intelligence_V519.html` in Edge or Chrome after extracting the complete ZIP.

Modules:
- **Food Intelligence**: 516 foods, search, category/status filters, research notes, compare up to three foods per 100 g, individual food PDF links.
- **Full Food Encyclopedia**: the *unchanged* V518 encyclopedia embedded in the integrated shell.
- **Research & Sources**: data coverage, source verification entry points, full JSON and CSV exports.

The 516 PDFs remain in `Food_PDFs/`, with paths matching the original V518 HTML.

**Evidence status**: 181 imported source profiles provide approximate, unverified nutrition values; 335 preliminary entries lack verified numeric tables. Research notes are not validated medical claims. No real photographs were added. The compare view does not rank healthiness or recommend medical treatment.

Original V518 and older releases remain unchanged.
