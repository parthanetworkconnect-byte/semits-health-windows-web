# SEMITS Health — Health Considerations V521

Separate copy of V9.14 + V520 Food Intelligence with **106** searchable health considerations in 8 groups.

Use START_WINDOWS.bat, select a family profile, then open Health considerations from the sidebar.

- Classify selected entries as self-reported diagnosed conditions, symptoms/concerns, or goals.
- Saves per family member in this browser under `semits_hc_v521_<profileId>` after pressing Save.
- Export JSON. This add-on does **not** automatically read medical records, access external health services, infer diagnoses, or generate tailored clinical dietary restrictions.
- Nutrient data in the 516-food research module are drafts, not clinically validated.
- Photo downloader and all 516 PDF exports remain unchanged.
- V9.14 and V520 original packages are not overwritten.

## Privacy and backup
Selections are stored locally in browser storage. They are **not included** in the original V9.14 general backup; use Export selected JSON for each profile. Avoid using this prototype on shared computers. This is not a securely authenticated health record.
