# SEMITS Health V9.5
- Introduces Healthy Living Mode (no medical records required): individualised rule-based seven-day starter movement, nutrition, sleep and habit report with official Australian guidance links.
- Adds 10–17, 18–64 and 65+ plan variants and safety flags for medicines, diagnosed conditions or exercise limitations.
- Allows TXT report download and saves wellness profiles under current family member.
- Medical extraction: attempts all scanned pages (up to 80), raises extracted text ceiling to 1.2M characters, supports structured findings view with page location and optional full-text TXT download.
- Retains human confirmation of Medical ID and does not make diagnoses or adjust medications.
- New local port 8773 to avoid older running instances; browser data at older ports must be exported and restored.
- Keeps original V9.4 modules and workflows.
