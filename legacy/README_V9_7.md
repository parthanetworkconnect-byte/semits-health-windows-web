# SEMITS Health V9.7 — 195-country food explorer

Run `START_WINDOWS.bat`, then open `http://127.0.0.1:8773` and choose **195-country foods** in the sidebar. Existing local profiles on this origin should remain available; back up before updating.

## Scope / limitations
- 195 named countries (193 UN members plus Palestine and Vatican City). 18 individually edited country examples plus 177 regional starter examples clearly marked. This does NOT constitute a verified national food ethnography for 195 countries.
- 27 common ingredients with rounded indicative nutrition per 100 g (not linked to verified USDA food IDs). Serving size calculator is illustrative.
- Cultural uses of foods are not medical evidence. No claimed cure or automated herbal interactions.
- Local crop provenance and traditional history need FAOSTAT and primary cultural research before any country-specific assertions.
- No live database synchronisation, research-scraping or AI inference is claimed.
- Existing V9.6 medical and healthy-living modules retained; no changes to medical data or medication instructions.
- Offline-first browser data is unencrypted. Avoid real medical records in an untrusted environment.
